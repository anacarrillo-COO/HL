Who we are

15+ years perfecting access at height

Our journey began working on all types of projects: from high-rise window cleaning using rope access and suspended scaffolding, to ground-level glass cleaning for homes and commercial properties.

After many years working for other companies, we decided to start our own with one clear goal: to provide professional-quality service backed by real experience and genuine dedication to the work we do. We saw firsthand how hard work and years of experience in this industry were often undervalued — and that motivated us to build something different.

Today we offer more complete solutions: residential and commercial window cleaning, pressure washing, soft washing, window sealing, leak testing, and other specialized exterior maintenance services.

How we work

The right method for every façade

Our flexibility in combining different access methods lets us serve almost any building.

MethodBest forKey advantage
Rope Access High-rise buildings where scaffolding or lifts cannot be used. Reaches hard-to-access areas without disrupting building operations.
Swing Stages & Lifts High-rise work on large-scale commercial façades. Broad, stable coverage for larger-volume projects.
Water-Fed Pole System Buildings up to 4–5 stories, without ropes or scaffolding. Safe work from the ground with exceptional cleaning quality.
