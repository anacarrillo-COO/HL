About this service

Swing Stages & Lifts

For large, flat commercial façades and scheduled maintenance contracts, swing stages and lifts often outperform rope access on sheer coverage. A suspended platform lets a crew work a wide section of glass at once, while boom and scissor lifts give precise, stable positioning at lower and mid-rise heights.

We combine this method with rope access and water-fed poles as needed, so a single building can be cleaned with whichever method fits each section best — instead of forcing one method to fit the whole job.

  • Suspended platform (swing stage) systems for wide façade coverage
  • Boom and scissor lift access for precise, stable positioning
  • Efficient for large, flat commercial façades
  • Well suited to scheduled, recurring maintenance contracts
