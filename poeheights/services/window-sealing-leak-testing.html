About this service

Window Sealing & Leak Testing

Water intrusion around windows is one of the most common (and most expensive) sources of building damage. We test windows for filtration and leaks, identify exactly where water is getting in, and correct it with a proper re-seal — rather than a temporary patch.

For property managers and engineers, we can provide documentation of findings and the work performed, useful for maintenance records, insurance, or tenant communication.

  • Leak and water-filtration testing
  • Professional re-sealing of windows and frames
  • Helps prevent costly, hidden water damage
  • Findings documented for property managers and building engineers
Ideal for
Buildings with reported leaksAging window systemsPre-purchase or pre-lease inspections
